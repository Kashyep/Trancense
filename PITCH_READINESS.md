# Pitch readiness

## Credible today

The implementation can demonstrate private evidence metadata/access, workspace roles, approved-input-only calculation logic, human review states, deterministic limitations, and authorized draft report generation. These are product capabilities, not third-party certifications or performance claims.

## Claims not to make

Do not claim customer adoption, savings, regulatory compliance/certification, AI capability, security certification, data residency, uptime, integrations, or market traction without supplied evidence.

## Missing proof/assets

See `CONTENT_REQUIRED.md`: final identity/contact/legal assets, approved screenshots, pilot workflow, substantiated trust proof, market evidence, pricing, and team material.

## One-page demo narrative

An auditor receives scattered monthly evidence. They create a boundary, upload private evidence, preserve raw values, review compatible records, inspect the calculation trail, turn a reviewed observation into an accountable action, and export a clearly limited draft report. The core message is traceability—not automation theatre.

## Recommended five-minute demo

1. Explain the evidence-to-verification chain (30s). 2. Show an audit boundary and raw-vs-normalized record (60s). 3. Approve a record and show what enters a KPI (60s). 4. Open calculation provenance and exclusions (60s). 5. Show finding → recommendation states (45s). 6. Generate/show the authorized draft report and limitations (45s).

## Pitch-site information architecture

Hero → workflow chain → evidence/traceability → roles → report workflow → security/human review → pilot contact. Add proof only when approved.

## Go/no-go

Public launch: NO-GO until approved identity, contact, legal, and deployment items are supplied. Pilot outreach: NO-GO until the security pre-deployment checklist and disposable Supabase tests pass. Investor pitch: NO-GO until factual proof, market, team, and business materials are supplied.
