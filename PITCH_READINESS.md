# Pitch readiness

## What can be credibly demonstrated today

- An evidence-first workflow: source evidence → approved data → reproducible calculation → reviewable finding → accountable action → measured verification.
- Workspace-scoped records, role-based access, private evidence metadata/access, and authorized report downloads.
- Raw-versus-normalized energy inputs, approved-input-only calculations, explicit missing-factor states, and auditable report exports.
- Draft/approval boundaries and recommendation states that keep Completed distinct from Verified.

These are implementation capabilities, not independent certifications, customer outcomes, or performance claims.

## What must not yet be claimed

Do not claim customer adoption, savings, regulatory compliance/certification, security certification, AI capability, data residency, uptime, integrations, market traction, or team credentials until the evidence in `CONTENT_REQUIRED.md` is supplied and approved.

## Missing proof and assets

Final identity, contact and legal material; approved screenshots; pilot workflow; customer permissioned proof; market sources; pricing; team material; and an approved contact route remain required. See `CONTENT_REQUIRED.md`.

## One-page demo narrative

An auditor begins with scattered monthly evidence. They define a reporting boundary, upload private evidence, retain original values, enter and review compatible records, inspect the calculation trail, turn a reviewed observation into an accountable action, and create an explicitly limited draft report. The message is traceability and professional judgement—not automation theatre.

## Recommended five-minute product demo

1. Explain the evidence-to-verification chain (30 seconds).
2. Show the audit boundary and raw-versus-normalized monthly record (60 seconds).
3. Approve a compatible record and explain what can enter a KPI (60 seconds).
4. Open calculation provenance, factors, warnings, and exclusions (60 seconds).
5. Show finding → recommendation state progression, including why Completed is not Verified (45 seconds).
6. Generate or show the authorized draft report and its limitations (45 seconds).

Hero → evidence-to-action chain → traceability/calculation detail → consultant and facility-reviewer roles → report workflow → security and human review → pilot contact. Add logos, metrics, testimonials, and case studies only after approval.
